### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** SOC313/SOC503  
**Course Name:** Sociology of India: Themes and Perspectives  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** PhD/UG  
**Course Description:** This course equips students with an understanding of the foundational concepts, contemporary debates, and key themes in Sociology and Social Anthropology within the Indian context. The primary objective is to prepare students for research in these disciplines by exploring theoretical frameworks, methodologies, and their application to socio-cultural issues in India.

---

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory:** None specified.  
**Desirable:** Basic understanding of sociological concepts.  
**Other:** None specified.

---

### Course Outcomes (COs)  
1. **CO1:** Students will be able to identify various sociological concepts, traditions, and themes relevant to India.
2. **CO2:** Students will contextualize sociological knowledge and research within Indian socio-cultural frameworks.
3. **CO3:** Students will understand the role of reflexivity and contextualization in sociological research.
4. **CO4:** Students will apply critical analytical approaches to study contemporary socio-cultural issues in India.
5. **CO5:** Students will develop an independent, reflexive, and critical approach towards formulating research problems.
