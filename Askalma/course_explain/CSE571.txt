### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE571  
**Course Name:** Introduction to Media Computing  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** UG/PG  
**Course Description:** This course introduces students to the fundamental principles, theory, algorithms, and data structures behind digital representation, compression, synchronization, and processing of image, audio, and video data types. It also explores challenges and issues in developing media-rich applications, such as media streaming and retrieval. Students will gain hands-on experience with common media file formats and manipulation techniques.

---

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory Pre-requisite:** None specified.  
**Desirable Pre-requisite:** Basic understanding of computer systems and programming concepts.  
**Other Pre-requisites:** None specified.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- **CO1:** Be confident in developing software and systems that capture, generate, store, and manipulate digital media.  
- **CO2:** Make appropriate design decisions to trade-off between different computation resources (CPU time, storage, bandwidth) and media quality in choosing a media representation and implementation.  
- **CO3:** Be equipped with fundamental knowledge to pursue multimedia-related topics in depth.  
- **CO4:** Use evaluation strategies to analyze results, prepare reports, and visually present insights.

### Course Outcomes (COs)  
The course outcomes are designed to ensure students gain a comprehensive understanding of digital media representation, compression techniques, and multimedia applications. By the end of the course, students should be able to:  
- Develop software systems for capturing, generating, storing, and manipulating digital media.  
- Make informed design decisions by balancing computation resources and media quality.  
- Apply fundamental knowledge in pursuing multimedia-related fields.  
- Analyze results, prepare reports, and present insights effectively.
