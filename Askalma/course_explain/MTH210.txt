### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** MTH210  
**Course Name:** Discrete Structures  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate students in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics  

This course provides an introduction to discrete mathematics, a foundational discipline for computer science and applied mathematics. Designed for undergraduate students, it equips learners with essential mathematical tools such as formal language, proof techniques, and discrete structures like graphs, permutations, and probability. The curriculum also integrates the probabilistic method to solve complex problems.

---

### Prerequisites  
- **Mandatory:** Discrete Mathematics (CSE121)  
- **Desirable:** None specified.  

Students are expected to have a basic understanding of mathematical concepts before enrolling in this course.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:  
1. **CO1:** Understand and express ideas using precise mathematical language.  
2. **CO2:** Develop proofs using various techniques such as direct proof, contrapositive, contradiction, and induction.  
3. **CO3:** Analyze discrete structures (e.g., graphs, combinatorial identities) and apply them to real-world problems.  
4. **CO4:** Introduce basic applications of discrete structures in computer science and mathematics.  
5. **CO5:** Apply the probabilistic method to solve problems across diverse domains.
