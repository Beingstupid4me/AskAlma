**Course Overview**

**Course Code:** SOC204  
**Course Name:** Sociology of New Media  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** UG  
**Course Description:** This course introduces students to macro-sociological concepts to understand the role of new media and online networking in contemporary society. It draws from sociological perspectives beyond 'technological determinism' to explore how IT-driven media influences social structures, including themes like inequality, digital divide, politics, journalism, and law.

**Prerequisites:** Introduction to Sociology

**Course Outcomes (COs):**

1. **CO1:** Students will grasp macro-level sociological theories and concepts related to new media and society.
2. **CO2:** Students will critically analyze assumptions in technological deterministic claims.
3. **CO3:** Students will explore how new media impacts various social structures, such as economic, community, and social capital dimensions.
4. **CO4:** Students will independently conduct research projects on new media-related topics.
5. **CO5:** Students will reflect critically on their practices as new media and social networking developers.

**Weekly Lecture Plan:**

1. **Week 1:** Introduction to moving beyond technological determinism
2. **Week 2:** New and old media: Sociological perspectives (PART I)
3. **Week 3:** Virtual community and social capital
4. **Week 4:** Networked individualism
5. **Week 5:** Information society in history
6. **Week 6:** Information society in history (continued)
7. **Week 7:** Information society in China
8. **Week 8:** Information society in India
9. **Week 9:** Media research: methods workshop
10. **Week 10:** New media and economy: Wikipedia (PART II)
11. **Week 11:** Social media and networked journalism
12. **Week 12:** Online rumours, bots, and violence
13. **Week 13:** Project presentations
14. **Week 14:** Revision and essay submission

**Assessment Plan:**

- **Response paper:** 10%
- **Mid-Term exam:** 20%
- **Final exam:** 30%
- **Class participation/quiz:** 10%
- **Project:** 30%
