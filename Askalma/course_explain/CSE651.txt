### Course Overview  
**Course Name:** Topics in Adaptive Cybersecurity (TAC)  
**Course Code:** CSE5TAC  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate/Postgraduate Students  
**Duration:** One academic semester  

This course is designed to provide students with an interactive learning platform focused on exploring advanced topics in adaptive cybersecurity. The primary objectives include instruction, hands-on projects, and student presentations of research papers. The curriculum covers essential concepts such as threat intelligence, situational awareness, and real-time adaptive security measures. By the end of this course, students will be equipped to analyze current trends, understand cutting-edge research, and contribute to ongoing discussions in the field of adaptive cybersecurity.

---

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory:**  
- **CSE345/CSE545 Foundations of Computer Security (or) CSE350/CSE550 Network Security**  

**Desirable:**  
- Students are expected to have a solid understanding of basic concepts in machine learning.  

**Other:**  
- None specified.

---

### Learning Outcomes  
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:  

1. **CO1:** Define and describe several key topics in adaptive cybersecurity.  
2. **CO2:** Deconstruct and explain the effectiveness and limitations of proposed solutions from relevant literature.  
3. **CO3:** Critique the effectiveness and limitations of solutions presented in academic literature.  
4. **CO4:** Design and develop specific solutions to address open problems in adaptive cybersecurity.  
5. **CO5:** Apply critical thinking and problem-solving skills to real-world scenarios in adaptive cybersecurity.
