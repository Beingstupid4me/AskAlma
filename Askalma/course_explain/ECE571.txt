### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** ECE571  
**Course Name:** Optimal Control Systems  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** UG/PG  
**Duration:** 1 Semester  

This course provides an advanced understanding of optimal control systems, focusing on the theoretical foundations and practical implementation techniques. It covers topics such as performance indices, variational approaches, Pontryagin minimum principle, and model predictive control. The course combines theoretical knowledge with problem-solving components to equip students with the skills to formulate, analyze, and solve optimal control problems in various fields like transportation, aerospace, bio-informatics, and economics.

---

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory Prerequisites:** None specified.  
**Desirable Prerequisites:** Strong foundation in calculus, linear algebra, and basic control systems concepts.  
**Other Prerequisites:** General academic readiness for undergraduate or postgraduate studies.  

Students are expected to have a solid understanding of fundamental engineering principles before enrolling in this course.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
1. **CO1:** Formulate an optimal control problem for different performance indices, including quadratic cost functions and tracking problems.  
2. **CO2:** Analyze optimal control problems using variational calculus, Pontryagin’s minimum principle, and Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equations.  
3. **CO3:** Solve linear and nonlinear optimization-based problems using open-source solvers like MATLAB or Python libraries.  
4. **CO4:** Implement and evaluate model predictive control strategies for constrained systems.

| Type of Evaluation | Contribution to Grade (%) |
|---------------------|---------------------------|
| Homework           | 20                        |
| Mid-sem            | 40                        |
