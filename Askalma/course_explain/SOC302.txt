**Course Overview**  
The course is an in-depth exploration of urban sociology, focusing on the evolution, characteristics, and challenges of cities. It integrates theoretical concepts with practical applications to provide a holistic understanding of urban phenomena.

**Prerequisites**  
Students must have completed **SOC101 (Introduction to Sociology & Anthropology)** as a mandatory prerequisite. Desirable prerequisites include familiarity with basic sociological theories and an interest in urban issues.

**Learning Outcomes**  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- Define and contextualize key concepts such as city, urbanism, and urbanization.  
- Identify and analyze urban spaces and phenomena.  
- Critically evaluate the role of technology in urban development and change.  
- Propose innovative solutions using technology for addressing urban challenges.
