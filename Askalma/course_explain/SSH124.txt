**Course Overview**

This course SSH124, titled "Critical Thinking" introduces the learner to the fundamentals of critical thinking and informal logic. It employs a mixed instructional methodology, combining lectures, Q&A sessions, learning team activities, video discussions, slides, print articles, and the primary textbook.

**Prerequisites**

- **Mandatory Pre-requisites**: None specified.
- **Desirable Pre-requisites**: None mentioned in the provided data.
- **Other Pre-requisites**: No specific information available.

**Learning Outcomes (COs)**

1. Demonstrate knowledge of underlying assumptions in statements, positions, and arguments.
2. Apply distinctions between perceptions, opinions, facts, values, and knowledge.
3. Define and recognize stereotypes, rationalization, prejudice, and their overlaps.
4. Practice active listening in any discussion.
5. Address 'loaded' issues with critical thinking.
6. Uncover common prejudices using semantic maps and territories.
7. Differentiate between Critical and Creative Thinking.
8. Identify and explain formal and informal logic fallacies.

**Weekly Syllabus and Topics**

1. **Week 1**: Introduction to critical thinking, distinction between Critical and Creative Thinking, identifying assumptions.
2. **Week 2**: Art of meaningful discussion, active listening, perception exercises.
3. **Week 3**: Discussion on opinion formation, learning team presentations.
4. **Week 4**: Exploration of 'truth' vs. 'fact', media bias samples.
5. **Week 5**: Stereotypes and generalizations, inductive logic discussion.
6. **Week 6**: David Hume's induction critique, group exercises.
7. **Week 7**: Multi-perspective thinking vs single perspective, critical thinking implications.
8. **Week 8**: General Semantics concepts, Hayakawa and Korzybski theories.
9. **Week 9**: Critical thinking exercises, research paper preparation.
10. **Week 10**: Po-beyond yes/no logic, research paper due.
11. **Week 11**: Innovation vs. critical thinking, reflective exercise.
12. **Week 12**: Fallacies analysis, deductive logic basics.
