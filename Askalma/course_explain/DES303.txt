### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** DES303  
**Course Name:** Film Making and Radio Podcasting  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) students  

This course is designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of film making and radio podcasting. It combines technical skills with creative processes, emphasizing the importance of editing in creating visually compelling narratives. Students will learn essential tools such as NLE software for audio and video editing, while also gaining hands-on experience in assembling podcasts and short video projects. The course is structured to build foundational knowledge in audio file formats, soundtracks, video shooting, and editing techniques, culminating in the creation of professional-grade final products.

---

### Prerequisites  
- **Mandatory:** Basic aesthetic ability to assemble films or podcasts.  
- **Desirable:** None specified.  
- **Other:** None specified.  

Students are encouraged to have a foundational understanding of visual storytelling but no prior experience with NLE software is required. The course will gradually build technical skills while focusing on creative problem-solving.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- **CO1:** Understand audio file formats and codecs, enabling them to arrange soundtracks, edit vocals, and create podcasts.  
- **CO2:** Arrange soundtracks, edit vocals, and create podcast episodes effectively.  
- **CO3:** Understand film shooting and editing concepts, including sequencing or editing video shots in a scene.  
- **CO4:** Shoot, capture, and output short video projects with technical precision.  
- **CO5:** Shoot, capture, and output short video projects independently, demonstrating mastery of the techniques covered in the course.
