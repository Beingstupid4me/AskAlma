**Course Overview**:  
ECE 554: Compressed Sensing is a senior-level course focusing on the fundamentals of Compressed Sensing (CS), its applications in signal processing, machine learning, and computer vision. The course aims to equip students with the ability to model physical problems using CS principles, implement algorithms in practical applications, understand research papers, and work on advanced projects.

**Prerequisites**:  
The prerequisite for this course is MTH100: Mathematics I. Additional prerequisites or other requirements can be inserted if needed.

**Learning Outcomes**:  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- **CO1**: Model physical problems (related to Compressed Sensing) using appropriate mathematical frameworks.  
- **CO2**: Implement Compressed Sensing algorithms in various applications and understand their limitations.  
- **CO3**: Understand and critically analyze current research papers published in top-tier journals and conferences related to Compressed Sensing.  
- **CO4**: Advanced project work in this area, demonstrating the ability to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world problems.  
- **CO5**: Present solutions and findings effectively, both in written and oral formats.
