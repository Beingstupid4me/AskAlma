**Course Overview**

This course CSE581, titled "Systems Analysis, Design and Requirements Engineering" is designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of Systems Analysis, Design, and Requirements Engineering. It focuses on the principles and practices essential for analyzing software systems, designing user-centered solutions, and managing requirements effectively.

**Prerequisites**

Basic understanding of software engineering and management is required. Students should have a foundational knowledge of software development processes and team collaboration.

**Learning Outcomes**

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- **CO1:** Identify key skills for analyzing software development processes.
- **CO2:** Differentiate between various approaches to systems analysis and design.
- **CO3:** Develop abilities in gathering data for recognizing, analyzing, and specifying system requirements.
- **CO4:** Recognize cognitive limitations in requirements determination and develop mitigation strategies.
- **CO5:** Identify appropriate Agile software development approaches.
