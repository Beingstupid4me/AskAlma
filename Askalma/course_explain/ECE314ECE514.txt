**Course Overview**

**Course Code:** ECE 314/ECE514  
**Course Name:** Digital VLSI Design  
**Credits:** 4  

This course introduces students to CMOS circuits, with a focus on developing first-order models for transistors, transfer characteristics of CMOS inverters, and performance estimation through logical effort, interconnects, combinational circuit design, sequential circuit design (including clocking and latching techniques), datapath subsystems (adders, shifters, multipliers), memory subsystems, and a course project using CAD tools in VLSI design.

**Prerequisites**

Students must have knowledge of digital logic, basic computer organization, and some understanding of RC circuits. These prerequisites are essential for grasping the advanced concepts covered in this course.

**Learning Outcomes (COs)**

By the end of this course, students will be able to:
- CO1: Evaluate characteristics of CMOS inverter and interconnects in terms of current and capacitance, estimating delay and power.
- CO2: Quickly estimate the best number of stages for a path, minimum possible delay, and optimal gate sizes using logical effort.
- CO3: Describe sources of power dissipation in CMOS chips and apply techniques to minimize power consumption.
- CO4: Optimize combinational circuits for lower delay or energy; analyze sequential circuits including clocking and latching techniques.
- CO5: Use CAD tools in VLSI design for state-of-the-art CMOS circuit development.
