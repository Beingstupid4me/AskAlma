# Course Overview

## Course Code and Name
The course is coded as SOC313/SOC503 and titled "Sociology of India: Themes and Perspectives." It is a 4-credit course offered to both PhD and undergraduate (UG) students.

## Course Description
This course provides an in-depth exploration of the foundational concepts, contemporary debates, and key themes in Sociology and Social Anthropology within the Indian context. The primary objective is to equip students with the necessary sociological knowledge and analytical skills required for conducting research in these fields. The course is divided into two main parts: the first focuses on theoretical foundations, methodologies, reflexivity, and contextualization, while the second part delves into specific themes such as caste, class, gender, religion, social relations, and social stratification.

## Prerequisites
There are no mandatory prerequisites for this course. However, it is recommended that students have a basic understanding of sociological concepts to fully benefit from the course content.

## Learning Outcomes (COs)
By the end of the course, students will be able to:
- **CO1:** Identify various Sociological Concepts, Themes, and Traditions.
- **CO2:** Contextually situate sociological knowledge and research within India.
- **CO3:** Develop a relevance of reflexivity and contextualization in sociological analysis.
- **CO4:** Apply a critical analytical lens to study various themes in Sociology.
- **CO5:** Construct an independent critical-reflexive approach toward research problems.
