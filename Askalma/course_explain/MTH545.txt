### Course Overview  
The course, **MTH545: Finite and Spectral Element Methods**, is an introductory program designed to provide students with a foundational understanding of the mathematical theory behind finite and spectral element methods for solving differential equations. The course emphasizes high-order numerical approximations and their applications in complex geometries. Students will gain insights into variational formulations, finite element techniques, and spectral element methods, as well as practical programming skills using these methodologies.

---

### Prerequisites  
To successfully engage with this course, students are expected to have completed the following prerequisites:  
- **Linear Algebra (MTH100)**  
- **Calculus (MTH203)** or **Real Analysis 1 (MTH240)**  
- **Differential Equations (MTH204)** or **Functional Analysis**  
Additionally, students should possess basic programming experience with any language. Desirable prerequisites include familiarity with numerical methods and an understanding of partial differential equations.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- **CO1**: Analyze the theory behind variational formulations of differential equations.  
- **CO2**: Understand and apply finite element and spectral element formulations for solving problems in one dimension.  
- **CO3**: Perform error analysis using high-order approximations.  
- **CO4**: Implement numerical solutions using low and high-order finite elements.

| 5    | Polynomial approximation theory    | CO2, CO3          | Assignment 5                |

### Learning Resources  
The course provides a comprehensive set of learning materials to support student success:  

1. **Textbook**:  
   - *An introduction to the mathematical theory of finite elements* by J.T. Oden and J.N. Reddy  
   - *Spectral methods: fundamentals in single domains* by C. Canuto, M.Y. Hussaini, A. Quarteroni, T.A. Zhang  

2. **Reference Books**:  
   - *An introduction to the finite element method* by J.N. Reddy  
   - *Functional analysis and applications* by S. Kesavan  

These resources will guide students through the theoretical foundations and practical implementations of finite and spectral element methods.
