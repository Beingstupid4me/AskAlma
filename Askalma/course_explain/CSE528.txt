### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE528  
**Course Name:** Introduction to Blockchain and Cryptocurrency  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) students  

This course provides an in-depth introduction to blockchain technology and its applications in cryptocurrency. It covers the foundational concepts, including how cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum function, the role of blockchain in decentralized consensus protocols, digital coin mining, security, privacy, scalability, and their practical uses in smart contracts and financial exchanges.

---

### Prerequisites  
- **Mandatory Pre-requisite:** Applied Cryptography ( recommended to have a solid understanding of cryptographic primitives such as encryption, hashing, and digital signatures).  
- **Desirable Pre-requisite:** None specified.  
- **Other Pre-requisites:** Basic knowledge of programming concepts is recommended for assignments involving smart contracts.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  

1. **CO1:** Understand how Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies operate in real-world scenarios.  
2. **CO2:** Grasp the cryptographic primitives that power blockchain infrastructure and cryptocurrency infrastructures.  
3. **CO3:** Anticipate future directions of cryptocurrency, including privacy, multiparty computation, and anonymity.
