**Course Overview**

**Course Code:** DES204  
**Course Name:** Human Computer Interaction  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** UG/PG  
**Course Description:** This course provides a comprehensive understanding of human computer interaction, focusing on user-centered design, modern interfaces, rapid prototyping, usability, and evaluation. It equips students with the skills to design intuitive and effective user interfaces.

**Prerequisites:** None specified  

**Learning Outcomes (COs):**  
- **CO1:** Broad understanding of HCI fundamentals and latest technologies.  
- **CO2:** Knowledge of user-centered design and rapid prototyping techniques.  
- **CO3:** Ability to evaluate multiple interface alternatives using quantitative and qualitative methods.  
- **CO4:** Proficiency in statistical analysis and effective presentation of research findings.
