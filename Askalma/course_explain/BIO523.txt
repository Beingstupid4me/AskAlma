### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** BIO523  
**Course Name:** Chemoinformatics  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate/Postgraduate (UG/PG) students  

This course provides an introduction to chemoinformatics, an interdisciplinary field at the intersection of chemistry, informatics, and biology. The primary objective is to equip students with a solid understanding of the fundamentals of chemoinformatics and its practical applications in areas such as drug discovery. Through a combination of lectures, hands-on exercises, and assignments, students will gain the ability to analyze and interpret data, apply computational tools, and solve real-world problems in this field.

---

### Prerequisites  
- **Knowledge of Chemistry:** Basic understanding of chemical principles and structures.  
- **Algorithms and Programming:** Familiarity with algorithms and programming concepts is desirable but not mandatory.  
- **Machine Learning:** A basic understanding of machine learning techniques will be beneficial for certain topics covered in the course.

---

### Course Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
1. **CO1:** Explain basic concepts of chemoinformatics and its relevance in modern scientific research.  
2. **CO2:** Implement computational tools for the calculation of molecular descriptors and analysis of chemical similarity.  
3. **CO3:** Classify small molecules based on structural and physicochemical properties, using chemoinformatics methods.  
4. **CO4:** Interpret results from chemoinformatics analyses to draw meaningful conclusions about molecular behavior and reactivity.
