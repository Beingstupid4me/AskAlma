**Course Overview**

- **Course Code:** CSE609  
- **Course Name:** Statistical Computation  
- **Credits:** 4  
- **Offered To:** UG/PG  
- **Course Description:** This course provides an in-depth understanding of statistical analysis methods, focusing on data collection, modeling, and inference. It covers both theoretical concepts and practical applications using computational tools.

**Prerequisites**

- **Mandatory:** MTH201 (Probability and Statistics)  
- **Desirable:** Basic knowledge of R or MATLAB programming  

**Learning Outcomes**

- **CO1:** Students will explain various statistical approaches and their applications.  
- **CO2:** Students will identify the relevance of different statistical methods for specific data analysis problems.  
- **CO3:** Students will utilize computational tools for statistical modeling.  
- **CO4:** Students will implement known statistical methods and critique results to draw conclusions.
