### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE562  
**Course Name:** Advanced Computer Vision  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** UG/PG  
**Course Description:** This course is designed to provide an in-depth understanding of advanced topics in computer vision. It covers essential concepts, techniques, and algorithms used in the field, with a focus on scalability issues and their practical applications in interdisciplinary domains such as robotics, healthcare, and forensics. Students will learn how to analyze complex visual data, develop robust solutions, and apply these skills to real-world problems. The course also emphasizes critical thinking and research-oriented learning, preparing students for advanced research or professional work in computer vision and related fields.

---

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory Prerequisites:** CSE344/CSE544/ECE344/ECE544  
**Desirable Prerequisites:** None specified.  

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- **CO1:** Understand the formulation and modeling of advanced computer vision problems.  
- **CO2:** Analyze scalability issues and implement solutions for large-scale datasets in computer vision.  
- **CO3:** Develop skills to analyze computer vision problems across various application domains (e.g., robotics, healthcare, forensics).  
- **CO4:** Understand the strengths and weaknesses of current approaches to research problems and identify future research directions.
