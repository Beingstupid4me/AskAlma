### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE694F/ECE651  
**Course Name:** Multimedia Security  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** UG/PG  
This course is designed to provide an in-depth understanding of multimedia security principles and practices. It covers a wide range of topics, including the fundamentals of digital media systems, various security techniques for multimedia content, and advanced concepts such as information hiding, steganography, and forensic analysis. The course also delves into the application of these concepts in real-world scenarios, with a focus on developing practical skills through assignments and laboratory exercises.

---

### Prerequisites  
- **Mandatory Pre-requisites:**  
  Students are required to have completed the following courses:  
  - CSE340/CSE540/ ECE350 Image Analysis/Digital Image Processing  
  - ECE250 Signals and Systems  
  - CSE344 Computer Vision  

- **Desirable Pre-requisites:**  
  Students with prior knowledge of Digital Signal Processing (DSP) or advanced programming skills in languages like MATLAB will have an advantage. Familiarity with multimedia content processing is also recommended.

---

### Learning Outcomes  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  

1. **CO1:** Understand and analyze the fundamental concepts of digital media systems and their security implications.  
2. **CO2:** Apply various media security techniques for protection against unauthorized access and tampering.  
3. **CO3:** Perform forensics and counter-forensics to detect and prevent multimedia content tampering.  
4. **CO4:** Demonstrate an understanding of visual surveillance systems and their applications in real-world scenarios.

### Assessment Strategy  
The course assessment is designed to evaluate both theoretical knowledge and practical skills. The breakdown of the evaluation is as follows:  

- **Quiz:** 20% (Includes both theoretical and application-based questions)  
- **Assignment:** 20% (Comprises both analytical and practical tasks)  
- **Mid-sem:** 15% (Assesses understanding of the first half of the course material)  
- **End-sem:** 20% (Evaluates comprehensive understanding of the entire course content)  
- **Project:** 25% (Focuses on a real-world application of multimedia security techniques)
