### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE663A  
**Course Name:** Meta-Learning  
**Credits:** 2  
**Offered To:** M.Tech, B.Tech 4th year, and PhD (CS).  
**Description:** Meta-Learning refers to the problem of developing machine learning/deep learning models that can adapt to new tasks/domains/environments with minimal retraining or without significant loss of performance. This course will focus on understanding the core concepts of meta-learning, its applications in industry, and its connections to broader AI research goals such as continual learning and robust generalization.  

### Prerequisites  
- **Mandatory:** AI, Machine Learning, or Deep Learning  
- **Desirable:** Familiarity with basic machine learning concepts and programming experience  
- **Other:** None specified  

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
1. Discuss the basic concepts of meta-learning and its applications in few-shot classification.  
2. Explain the core challenges in out-of-distribution generalization and causality in machine learning, and how meta-learning addresses these challenges.  
3. Understand and apply logical inductive biases via meta-interpretive learning to achieve certain goals of meta-learning.  
4. Apply meta-learning techniques to practical problems in e-commerce, advertising, and finance.
