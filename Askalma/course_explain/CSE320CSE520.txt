### Course Overview

**Course Code:** CSE320/CSE520  
**Course Name:** Advanced Algorithms  
**Credits:** 4  

This graduate-level course is designed to provide an in-depth exploration of advanced algorithmic techniques. The curriculum focuses on both theoretical foundations and practical applications, with a significant emphasis on developing skills in algorithm design, analysis, and optimization. Students are expected to engage with complex topics such as string matching, polynomial algorithms, numerical methods, primality testing, flow networks, and linear programming. Additionally, the course includes an optional graduate project that requires students to write a survey paper.

---

### Prerequisites

**Mandatory Prerequisites:**  
- CSE102: Advanced Data Structures and Algorithms  

**Desirable Prerequisites:** None specified.  

Students are expected to have a strong foundation in basic algorithms and data structures, as well as proficiency in mathematical proofs and problem-solving techniques.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)

By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- **CO1:** Understand higher-level theoretical concepts used in computer science.  
- **CO2:** Develop skills in analyzing and mathematically proving algorithmic facts.  
- **CO3 (Graduate version):** Be able to appreciate and understand theoretical sections of research papers.
