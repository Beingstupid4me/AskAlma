### Course Overview

**Course Code:** CSE735  
**Course Name:** Advanced Topics in Mobile Computing  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate/Postgraduate (UG/PG) students  

This course is designed to provide an in-depth understanding of advanced topics in mobile computing. The primary focus will be on recent advancements and research papers that highlight technologies such as sensing, activity inference, mobile communications, road and traffic sensing, and participatory sensing systems. Students will gain insights into how these technologies can be integrated to create next-generation mobile computing systems.

---

### Course Prerequisites

- **Mandatory Pre-requisite:** CSE232 Computer Networks  
- **Desirable Pre-requisites:** Operating System, Mobile Computing or Mobile Programming  

Note: Additional pre-requisites may be required if necessary. Please consult the instructor for further details.

---

### Course Outcomes (COs)

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. **CO1:** Identify and analyze the key challenges and issues in mobile computing domains.
2. **CO2:** Critique and evaluate research papers related to mobile computing technologies.
3. **CO3:** Design and develop advanced mobile computing solutions using various components such as sensors, communication protocols, and data analysis techniques.
4. **CO4:** Integrate multiple components of mobile computing to create robust and efficient systems for real-world applications.

### Assessment Plan

The course assessment is divided as follows:

- **Written paper critiques:** 35%  
- **Class participation and presentation:** 15%  
- **Project work:** 40%  
- **Endsem (end-of-semester examination):** 10%  

Additional assessments may be included at the discretion of the instructor.

This course offers a comprehensive exploration of advanced mobile computing technologies.
