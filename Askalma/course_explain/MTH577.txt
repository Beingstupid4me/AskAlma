### Course Overview  
The course titled **Convex Optimization** (Course Code: MTH377/MTH577) is designed for both undergraduate and postgraduate students. It provides an in-depth exploration of convex optimization problems, their mathematical theory, and efficient algorithms. The course emphasizes the foundational concepts of convex sets and functions, which serve as a cornerstone for understanding and solving various real-world optimization challenges across disciplines such as economics, operations research, and engineering. The primary objective is to equip students with the theoretical knowledge and practical skills to recognize, formulate, and solve convex optimization problems effectively.

---

### Prerequisites  
The course requires **MTH100** (Calculus) and **MTH203** (Linear Algebra) as mandatory prerequisites. Students should have a solid understanding of basic calculus concepts and linear algebra principles before enrolling in this course. Additional prerequisite details or materials are available upon request.

---

### Learning Outcomes  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- **CO1:** Understand and apply the structure theory of convex sets and functions.  
- **CO2:** Formulate and solve convex optimization problems using appropriate algorithms.  
- **CO3:** Recognize and utilize duality theory in convex optimization.  
- **CO4:** Analyze sensitivity and perform complementary slackness analysis for optimal solutions.  
- **CO5:** Apply convex optimization techniques to real-world problems such as least squares approximation, linear classification, portfolio choice models, and principal agent models.

### Assessment Plan  
The course assessment is designed to evaluate both theoretical understanding and practical application of convex optimization concepts. The evaluation components include:  

- **Coding Assignment:** 20% (Assesses problem-solving skills using computational tools)  
- **Quiz:** 20% (Evaluates understanding of fundamental concepts)  
- **Midterm Exam:** 30% (Comprehensive assessment of material covered in the first half of the course)  
- **Endterm Examination:** 30% (In-depth evaluation of advanced topics and overall understanding)

The end-term examination carries a significant weightage, reflecting its importance in assessing students' mastery of the subject matter.
