**Course Overview**  
The course titled "Discrete Mathematics" (Course Code: CSE121) is a 4-credit structured program offered to undergraduate students. It serves as a foundational course for computer science, focusing on discrete structures that are essential in modern computation and cryptography.

---

**Prerequisites**  
Students must have a solid understanding of elementary mathematics, including algebra, calculus, and basic programming concepts. Additionally, completion of Discrete Structures (MTH210) is recommended to ensure readiness for the course content.

---

**Learning Outcomes (COs)**  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- **CO1:** Understand and apply basic mathematical notations in discrete mathematics.  
- **CO2:** Identify and construct examples of mathematical objects such as sets, functions, relations, and graphs.  
- **CO3:** Develop mathematical models using discrete structures to solve real-world problems.  
- **CO4:** Apply problem-solving techniques and critical thinking skills to analyze complex scenarios.

**Assessment Strategy**  
The course employs a comprehensive assessment strategy:  
- **Homework:** Contributes to the final grade and reinforces learning.  
- **Quiz:** Regular assessments to evaluate understanding of key concepts.  
- **Mid-sem:** A mid-semester examination covering the first half of the syllabus.  
- **End-sem:** A cumulative end-semester examination contributing significantly to the final grade.  

The remaining 5% is allocated for participation in class discussions or group projects.

**Resource Materials**

1. **Textbook:** Kenneth H. Rosen, *Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications*, 7th Edition, McGraw-Hill Education.  
2. **Reference Book:** Kolman, Busby, Ross, *Discrete Mathematical Structures*, PHI Learning Pvt. Ltd.

These resources are essential for understanding the course content and completing assignments successfully.
