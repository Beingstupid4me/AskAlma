**Course Overview: Basic Electronics**

**Course Code**: ECE113  
**Course Name**: Basic Electronics  
**Credits**: 4  
**Offered To**: Undergraduate (UG) students  

---

### **Course Description**
This course introduces the foundational concepts of electrical circuits, focusing on circuit elements such as resistors, capacitors, and inductors. It covers essential topics including Kirchhoff’s laws, network theorems, transient responses, steady-state analysis, operational amplifiers, and diode-based circuits. The curriculum is designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of both theoretical principles and practical applications.

### **Prerequisites**
- **Mandatory**: None specified.
- **Desirable**: Basic high school physics knowledge for a smooth learning experience.

### **Learning Outcomes (COs)**
By the end of this course, students will be able to:
1. Describe ideal circuit elements and their non-ideal behaviors.
2. Explain and apply Kirchhoff’s Laws and Network Theorems in problem-solving.
3. Analyze RC, RL, and RLC circuits under various conditions (transient and steady-state).
4. Classify and implement operational amplifier configurations.
5. Understand diode operation and design basic rectifier and filter circuits.
