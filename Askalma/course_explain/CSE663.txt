### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE663  
**Course Name:** Edge AI  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate/Postgraduate (UG/PG)  

This course is designed to provide an in-depth understanding of On-Device Artificial Intelligence (Edge AI), focusing on the development and implementation of intelligent systems directly on edge devices. The curriculum integrates advanced concepts from machine learning, deep learning, and cloud computing to prepare students for real-world applications in IoT, smart cities, and industrial automation.

---

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory Pre-requisites:** Machine Learning (CSE 343/543) or equivalent experience with Python-based machine learning frameworks like TensorFlow. A basic understanding of neural networks is highly recommended.  

**Desirable Pre-requisites:** Familiarity with cloud computing platforms and exposure to edge computing technologies would be advantageous but are not mandatory.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
1. **CO1:** Understand and explain the fundamental concepts of Edge AI and its applications in IoT and industry 4.0.  
2. **CO2:** Design and implement intelligent systems on edge devices using machine learning and deep learning techniques.  
3. **CO3:** Evaluate different frameworks and tools for deploying Edge AI solutions efficiently.  
4. **CO4:** Analyze real-world problems and propose innovative solutions leveraging On-Device AI technologies.
