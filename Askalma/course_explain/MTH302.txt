**Course Overview**

This course MTH302, titled "Algebra" introduces the core algebraic structures: groups, rings, and fields. It provides a foundational understanding of these structures, emphasizing both abstract concepts and concrete examples. The course is designed to enhance mathematical rigor, problem-solving skills, and proof-writing abilities.

---

**Prerequisites**

- **Mandatory**: MTH 100 or equivalent (Linear Algebra)

---

**Learning Outcomes (COs)**

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. **CO1**: Explain algebraic properties of groups, rings, and fields.
2. **CO2**: Classify groups up to isomorphism.
3. **CO3**: Discuss algebraic properties of rings and fields, including finite fields.
4. **CO4**: Write formal mathematical proofs.
5. **CO5**: Apply algebraic concepts to model and solve problems in communication and computer science.
