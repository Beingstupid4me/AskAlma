**Course Overview: Technical Communication**

This course COM301A, titled "Technical Communication" is designed as a two-credit, practice-oriented module focused on enhancing technical writing skills. It emphasizes critical thinking and effective structuring, guiding students through various aspects of professional communication. The curriculum includes exploring principles of technical writing, analyzing professional samples, engaging in research paper drafting, poster design, presentations, and developing resumes and cover letters.

**Prerequisites:**

The prerequisite for this course is **Course Code 101**, which typically corresponds to second-year standing or higher. Students are expected to have a foundational understanding of academic writing and communication principles before enrolling.

**Course Outcomes (COs):**

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- **CO1:** Articulate technical information with clear logical progression, eliminating unnecessary clutter.
- **CO2:** Effectively draft short technical reports using rubrics discussed in class.
- **CO3:** Employ reading strategies and mind mapping techniques for better comprehension of research papers.
- **CO4:** Avoid plagiarism by appropriately citing sources and justify their writing choices.
- **CO5:** Apply communication strategies to formal documents such as cover letters, emails, and resumes.

**Weekly Syllabus:**

1. **Session 1:** Introduction to technical writing principles with a focus on avoiding clutter through class activities.
2. **Session 2:** Drafting short technical reports using rubrics from previous sessions.
3. **Session 3:** Reading research papers and creating mind maps for better understanding.
4. **Session 4:** Conducting literature surveys, drafting review notes, and discussing plagiarism pitfalls.
5. **Session 5:** Writing abstracts and research pitches as part of a short research paper module.
6. **Session 6:** Continuing work on research papers, including methodology, analysis, and conclusions.
7. **Session 7:** Designing posters based on ongoing research projects.
8. **Session 8:** Preparing resumes and cover letters for professional communication.

**Assessment Plan:**

The course is assessed through various components contributing to the final grade:

- **Short scientific report (10%)**
- **Review (10%)**
- **Abstract (10%)**
- **Research pitch (10%)**
- **Short research paper (25%)**
- **Poster presentation (15%)**
- **Formal emails/resume (10%)**

**Total Contribution:** 100%

**Resource Materials:**

Lecture slides and recommended readings are provided for each session. Additional resources include articles, journal papers, and guides to support learning.

**Additional Engagement:**

Beyond class sessions, students engage in:

- Completing similar reports based on previously published scientific experiments.
- Individual reading assignments and annotations.
- Participating in review article discussions and outlining research papers.
- Collaborative work on drafting short research papers, posters, and formal communications like emails and resumes.
