### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** SOC202  
**Course Name:** Contemporary India: Sociological Perspective  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate (UG) students  

This course provides an in-depth exploration of contemporary issues in India, focusing on its sociocultural, political, economic, and technological dimensions. Grounded in sociology, the course integrates insights from other social sciences to critically examine India's transformation over time. Students will gain a comprehensive understanding of India's developmental challenges and fault lines while developing analytical skills to interpret diverse societal phenomena.

---

### Prerequisites  
No specific prerequisites are required for this course. Students are expected to have a basic understanding of sociological concepts, though familiarity with social sciences is advantageous.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  

- **CO1:** Analytically examine the social foundations of Indian society and comprehend diverse socio-cultural experiences.  
- **CO2:** Understand the wide socio-cultural diversity in India and its implications for various population segments based on caste, class, ethnicity, age, gender, and religion.  
- **CO3:** Critique the changes in Indian society during globalization and rapid economic change.  
- **CO4:** Assess and interpret the social impact of media and digital culture on Indian dynamics.  
- **CO5:** Critically examine inherent tensions and negotiations in India's development processes.
