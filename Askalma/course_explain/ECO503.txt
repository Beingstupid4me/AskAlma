### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** ECO503  
**Course Name:** Decision Theory  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate and Postgraduate (UG/PG)  

This course provides a comprehensive foundation in rational decision-making under both certainty and uncertainty. It explores the theoretical underpinnings of human behavior through various models, including utility theory, game theory, and behavioral economics. The course is designed to equip students with analytical tools to evaluate decision-making processes in diverse contexts such as economics, finance, and public policy.

---

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory Pre-requisites:** None specified.  
**Desirable Pre-requisites:** A strong foundation in basic mathematics and an introductory course in microeconomics are recommended for this course.  

Students who lack these prerequisites may find it challenging to keep up with the advanced topics covered in this course.

---

### Course Outcomes (COs)  
1. **CO1:** Students will be able to understand the foundational principles of decision-making under subjective uncertainty and apply them to real-world scenarios.  
2. **CO2:** Students will master the techniques for analyzing decisions under objective uncertainty using models like expected utility theory.  
3. **CO3:** Students will demonstrate proficiency in applying rational choice theories to substantive applications, such as portfolio selection and insurance choices.  
4. **CO4:** Students will understand how to use decision rules to analyze complex problems involving multiple objectives or sequential decisions.  
5. **CO5:** Students will appreciate the role of statistical methods in decision-making processes.
