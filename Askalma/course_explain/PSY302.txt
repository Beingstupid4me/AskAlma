### Course Overview  
This is an introductory-level course on Social Psychology (Course Code: PSY302) designed for undergraduate students. The course spans 15 weeks and carries 4 academic credits. It provides a foundational understanding of Social Psychology, focusing on key theories, research methods, and real-world applications. Students will explore topics such as individual behavior in social contexts, group dynamics, decision-making processes, and the impact of social interactions on emotions and relationships.

---

### Prerequisites  
Students are required to have completed an introductory course in Psychology (Introduction to Psychology) before enrolling in this course. This prerequisite ensures that students have a basic understanding of psychological principles, which will be built upon in this course.

---

### Course Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- **CO1:** Describe the origins and scope of Social Psychology, distinguishing it from other psychological disciplines.  
- **CO2:** Compare and contrast various theories related to social behavior, as well as the research methods used to investigate social psychology problems.  
- **CO3:** Apply theoretical knowledge and research skills to analyze real-world social phenomena.  
- **CO4:** Develop a research proposal addressing a novel question in Social Psychology.

### Assessment Strategy  
The course assessment is designed to evaluate both theoretical knowledge and practical application skills. The breakdown is as follows:  

| Type of Evaluation         | % Contribution to Final Grade | Coursework/Weighting Notes                          |
|-----------------------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| Homework Assignments        | 15%                           | Weekly assignments to reinforce concepts learned in class.      |
| Exam 1                      | 20%                           | Covers foundational theories and research methods.          |
| Exam 2                      | 20%                           | Focuses on advanced topics such as group behaviour and social influence.    |
| Exam 3                      | 20%                           | Comprehensive assessment of all course material.            |
| In-class Quizzes/Participation | 10%                          | Weekly quizzes to assess understanding of key concepts.       |
| Final Project               | 15%                           | A research project exploring a contemporary issue in Social Psychology. |

### Conclusion  
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to Social Psychology, equipping students with the knowledge and skills to analyze and understand social behaviour in various contexts. Through a combination of lectures, discussions, assignments, and assessments, students will gain a deep understanding of the field's theoretical foundations, research methods, and practical applications.
