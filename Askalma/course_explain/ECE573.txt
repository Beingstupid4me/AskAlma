**Course Overview**

**Course Code:** ECE 573  
**Course Name:** Advanced Embedded Logic Design  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** UG/PG  
**Course Description:** This course delves into embedded systems utilizing multi-core processors, accelerators like GPU and FPGA, and co-processors. The curriculum combines theoretical concepts with hands-on lab work to design real-world embedded systems. Key topics include processor architecture, interrupts, DMA, AXI protocol, and hardware-software co-design approaches for optimizing area and power performance.

**Learning Outcomes:**  
- Students will understand the fundamentals of embedded system design.
- They will master hardware-software co-design techniques on SoC platforms (Zybo, Zedboard, ZC706).
- Students will implement machine learning algorithms on SoC using dynamic partial reconfiguration for real-time applications.

**Weekly Syllabus & Topics**

1. **Week 1 & Week 2:**  
   - Introduction to embedded systems and AXI protocol.
   - Lab exercises: Basics of SoC design.

2. **Week 3:**  
   - FPGA refresh, IP design.
   - Lab exercises: FPGA implementation.

3. **Week 4 & Week 5:**  
   - SoC design flow, ARM+FPGA architecture.
   - Labs: Designing on Zynq SoC.

4. **Week 6 & Week 7:**  
   - Interrupts, timers, DMA systems.
   - Labs: Implementing FIFO and memory operations.

5. **Week 8 & Week 9:**  
   - Co-processors, FPU, clock systems.
   - Labs: Floating-point unit implementation.

6. **Week 10:**  
   - Reconfigurable architectures.
   - Labs: Dynamic partial reconfiguration.

7. **Week 11:**  
   - Accelerator design (FFT, matrix operations).
   - Labs: ML algorithm implementation.

8. **Week 12 & Week 13:**  
   - Zynq MPSoC architecture.
   - Labs: Designing on Zynq MPSoC.

**Assessment Plan**

- **Mid-sem Evaluation:** Lab and theory combined (30%).
- **Project:** 25% contribution.
- **Quiz (Theory):** 10%.
- **Quiz (Lab):** 10%.
- **End-sem Evaluation:** Lab and theory combined (25%).
