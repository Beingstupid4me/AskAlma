### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** ECE 516  
**Course Name:** System on Chip Design and Test  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** UG/PG  
**Course Description:** This course introduces students to the design trends in deep submicron (DSM) era, including scaling trends, clock cycle and power issues. It covers the role of interconnects in contemporary SoC Design, system-on-chip (SoC) and platform-based design, IP-based design for reusability, multiprocessor SoC platform design, 3D integration, on-chip optical communication, and wireless communication. Students will learn about design challenges, testability, and hands-on assignments to reinforce theoretical concepts.

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory Prerequisites:**  
- Introduction to VLSI Design  
- Basic Computer Organization and Architecture  

**Desirable Prerequisites:** None specified.  

**Other Prerequisites (if any):** None specified in the provided data.  

### Learning Outcomes  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
1. **CO1:** Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of design trends, interconnects, platform-based design, reusability, and power distribution networks.  
2. **CO2:** Understand concepts related to Test Access Mechanism (TAM), core-based testing, and IEEE P1500 standards for SoC test.  
3. **CO3:** Analyze, simulate, and design complex CMOS circuits/sub-systems with a focus on low power and high-speed performance.

### Assessment Strategy  
The course is assessed through a combination of assignments, quizzes, mid-semester and end-semester examinations, and a final project. The weightage distribution is as follows:  
- **Homework:** 5%  
- **Quizzes:** 5%  
- **Reading Assignments:** 15%  
- **Mid-Semester Examination:** 20%  
- **End-Semester Examination:** 25%  
- **Project:** 30%
