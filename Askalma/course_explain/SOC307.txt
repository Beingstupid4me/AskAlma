**Course Overview**  
The course titled "Digital Social Research" (SOC307) is a 4-credit module designed for undergraduate students. This course provides an in-depth exploration of digital social research methods and their applications. Students will learn about the theoretical underpinnings, practical methodologies, and ethical considerations associated with conducting research using digital data. The course aims to equip learners with the skills necessary to formulate research questions, design studies, collect and analyze data, and interpret results effectively.

**Prerequisites**  
- **Mandatory**: Introduction to Social Research  
- **Desirable**: Basic Computer Proficiency  
- **Other**: None specified  

**Learning Outcomes (Course Outcomes)**  
1. **CO1**: Students will be able to distinguish method and design, and apply research techniques specific to digital data collection and analysis.  
2. **CO2**: The students will be able to assess the significance of digital data for social research vis-à-vis conventional social research.  
3. **CO3**: The students will learn about the ethical issues related to digital social research and how to address them.  
4. **CO4**: The course will enable the students to develop research proposals that make appropriate use of the learnt methods.
