### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE605A  
**Course Name:** Knowledge Graphs in Practice  
**Credits:** 2  
**Offered To:** 3rd, 4th year UG, PG, PhD students.  

This is a hands-on, seminar-style course designed to provide an in-depth understanding of knowledge graphs and their practical applications. The course will cover the fundamentals of knowledge graphs, including their construction, querying, and optimization techniques. Students will also explore real-world use cases such as search engines, question-answering systems, chatbots, and enterprise data management.  

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory:**  
- CSE508 or CSE551 (Information Retrieval/NLP)  
- Knowledge of Java/Python programming  

**Desirable:**  
- Familiarity with machine learning concepts  
- Basic understanding of the semantic web  

**Other:**  
No specific prerequisites are mentioned beyond the mandatory ones.  

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
1. **CO1:** Understand basic principles of knowledge graph technology and describe the components of a knowledge graph.  
2. **CO2:** Explain the steps involved in knowledge graph construction, including challenges and methods for overcoming them.  
3. **CO3:** Provide hands-on experience with practical applications such as search engines, question-answering systems, chatbots, and industry-specific use cases like healthcare and drug discovery.

### Assessment Strategy  
The course will be assessed through the following components:  
1. **Course Project:** 50% of the grade (including design, implementation, and presentation)  
2. **Paper Reading, Presentation, and Discussion:** 20% of the grade (covering key papers related to knowledge graphs and their applications)  
3. **End Semester Examination:** 30% of the grade (comprehensive exam covering all course topics)
