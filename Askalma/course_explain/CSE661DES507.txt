### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE661/DES507  
**Department:** Computer Science and Engineering  
**Course Name:** Affective Computing  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** UG/PG  
**Course Description:** This course focuses on enabling machines with emotion recognition and adaptive interaction, bridging the intersection of Computer Science, design, and Human Psychology. It covers the theoretical foundations, computational modeling, and practical applications of emotions in computing, including voice, facial expressions, physiological signals, machine learning, and signal processing techniques. Ethical, legal, and social implications are also explored in relation to Human-Machine Interaction.

---

### Prerequisites  
- **Mandatory:** CSE101: Introduction to Programming, CSE102: Data Structures and Algorithms, CSE201: Advanced Programming, DES519: Design of Interactive Systems.  
- **Desirable:** CSE343/CSE543: Machine Learning, CSE643: Artificial Intelligence, DES204: Human-Computer Interaction.  
- **Other:** None specified.

---

### Course Outcomes (COs)  
1. **CO1:** Students will demonstrate an understanding of the state-of-the-art concepts in Computer Science, design, and psychology related to Affective Computing.  
2. **CO2:** Students will implement, compare, and evaluate different affect analysis methods individually or in groups.  
3. **CO3:** Students will design a method for affect analysis (including data collection, design, development, and evaluation) in at least one field of Affective Computing.  
4. **CO4:** Students will predict the potential consequences of Affective Computing and propose ethical, legal, and social guidelines for its development.
