# Course Overview  
**Course Code:** ECE579  
**Course Name:** Nonlinear and Adaptive Control of Robotic Systems  
**Credits:** 4  

---

### **1. Course Description**  
This course provides an introduction to nonlinear dynamical systems and emphasizes the role of control theory in robotics engineering. The curriculum includes modeling robotic systems, designing controllers for trajectory tracking applications, discussing advanced nonlinear and adaptive control strategies for fully-actuated robot manipulators and underactuated systems like surface vehicles and drones. Theoretical analysis using Lyapunov stability theory is also covered, along with practical demonstrations through live demos of control design on a Rotary Inverted Pendulum.

---

### **2. Prerequisites**  
- A basic familiarity with Linear Systems and Linear Algebra Control Theory (ECE570) or analogous Classical Control (ECE571).  

---

### **3. Learning Outcomes (COs)**  
Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:  
- Understand the fundamentals of nonlinear dynamical systems and control.  
- Model robotic systems and design nonlinear controllers.  
- Analyze controllers while ensuring stability and performance requirements are met.  
- Design nonlinear controllers for fully-actuated robot manipulators using model-based approaches.  
- Apply adaptive control techniques to handle real-time uncertainties in robotic systems.  
- Design synchronization strategies for multi-robot systems, including bilateral tele-operation and leader-follower formation control.
