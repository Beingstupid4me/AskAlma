### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE 546  
**Course Name:** Applied Cryptography  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate and Postgraduate students (UG/PG)  

This course provides an in-depth exploration of cryptography, focusing on both theoretical foundations and practical applications. Students will gain a comprehensive understanding of cryptographic principles and their real-world implementation. The course assumes a basic familiarity with mathematics and computer science concepts but does not require prior expertise in cryptography.

---

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory Pre-requisite:** CSE 121 Discrete Mathematics  

**Desirable Pre-requisites:** None specified  

Students are expected to have a foundational understanding of discrete mathematics, as this course builds upon these concepts. Additional pre-requisites can be inserted if necessary.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  

1. **CO1:** Analyze weak ciphers and break them using various cryptographic techniques.  
2. **CO2:** Summarize the design principles of block ciphers, stream ciphers, hash functions, and public key cryptography schemes.  
3. **CO3:** Explain why certain public key schemes (e.g., RSA and El Gamal) work and their underlying mathematical principles.  
4. **CO4:** Apply cryptographic protocols to solve real-world security challenges.

### Assessment Plan  

The course assessment is designed to evaluate both theoretical understanding and practical application. The breakdown is as follows:  

- **Project:** 20% (Includes a final project where students design and implement cryptographic solutions)  
- **Assignments:** 20% (Weekly assignments to reinforce lecture concepts)  
- **Mid-Semester Examination:** 25% (Evaluates understanding of core concepts covered in the first half of the course)  
- **End-Semester Examination:** 35% (Comprehensive assessment of all course material)  

Additional assessments can be inserted if necessary.
