### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** DES518  
**Course Name:** Introduction to Motion Graphics  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate/Postgraduate (UG/PG)  
**Course Description:** This course introduces students to the fundamentals of motion graphics, including the principles and tools used in its production. Students will learn how to define functions, understand the significance of motion graphics, and utilize software tools like Adobe After Effects, Photoshop, and Illustrator for creating visual content. The course also includes hands-on assignments and projects to apply these concepts effectively.

---

### Prerequisites  
- **Mandatory Prerequisites:**  
  Students must have a basic skill in graphic design tools such as **Illustrator** and **Photoshop**.  

- **Desirable Prerequisites (Optional):** None specified.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
1. **CO1:** Understand the basics of motion graphics, including its definition and significance.  
2. **CO2:** Acquire knowledge of essential tools used in motion graphics production, such as Adobe After Effects, Photoshop, and Illustrator.  
3. **CO3:** Develop skills in designing typographic elements, visualization for motion graphics, and creating animations.  
4. **CO4:** Design a short visual story from a given script using animation techniques.
