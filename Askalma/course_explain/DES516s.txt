### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** DES516s  
**Course Name:** Introduction to 3D Animation  
**Credits:** 2  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate/Postgraduate (UG/PG)  
**Course Description:** This course introduces students to the fundamentals of animation, focusing on both traditional and digital techniques. Students will learn about the basics of animation workflows, character modeling, and animation principles using Maya software. The course also covers advanced topics such as posing, movement, and technical aspects of animation.

---

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory Prerequisites:** None specified.  
**Desirable Prerequisites:** Basic understanding of computer graphics and familiarity with 3D modeling tools.  
**Other Prerequisites:** Students are expected to have access to Maya software for assignments and projects.

---

### Learning Outcomes (Course Outcomes)  
1. **CO1:** Understanding the basic workflow and animation principles.  
2. **CO2:** Getting familiar with Maya tools for animation.  
3. **CO3:** Implementing animation fundamentals and Maya techniques to create character animations.
