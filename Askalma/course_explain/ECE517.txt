**Course Overview**

The course ECE517, titled "Introduction to Nanoelectronics," is a 4-credit offering designed for both undergraduate and postgraduate students. This interdisciplinary course delves into the fundamentals of nanoelectronic devices, exploring their operation, electrical characteristics, and recent advancements in the field.

**Course Prerequisites**

To successfully engage with this course, students should have a basic understanding of P-N junctions and MOSFETs. These foundational concepts are crucial for grasping the more intricate topics covered throughout the semester.

**Learning Outcomes (COs)**

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- **CO1:** Clearly explain distinct phenomena that are important in nanoelectronic devices.
- **CO2:** Describe the operating principles, merits, demerits, and challenges of various futuristic nanoelectronic devices.
- **CO3:** Compute a given parameter or physical quantity for a nanoelectronic device by applying appropriate equations or formula.
- **CO4:** Discuss the challenges of scaling conventional MOSFETs and possible solutions.

**Weekly Lecture Plan**

The course is structured over 13 weeks, with each week focusing on specific topics:

1. **Week 1:** Introduction to nanoelectronics, bottom-up approach, trends, and ITRS perspective.
2. **Week 2:** Quantum mechanics basics: Particle description, Schrödinger’s equation, expectation value, particle in a box, finite potential well.
3. **Week 3:** Energy bands, density of states, distribution functions, Fermi level, quantum wells, wires, and dots.
4. **Week 4:** Transport in confined materials, ballistic transport.
5. **Week 5:** Effects of subthreshold swing limitations and their consequences.
6. **Week 6:** Strain effects, FinFETs, III-V materials.
7. **Week 7:** 2-D materials: Graphene, transition metal dichalcogenides (TMD).
8. **Week 8:** 1-D materials: Carbon nanotubes and nanowires.
9. **Week 9:** CNT-based FETs, graphene nanoribbon-based FETs.
10. **Week 10:** Steep subthreshold slope devices: Tunnel FET, NCFET, IMOS.
11. **Week 11:** Nanoelectromechanical switches (NEMS).
12. **Week 12:** Novel devices: Single electron transistors and molecular devices.
13. **Week 13:** Summing up and presentation.

**Assessment Strategy**

The course assessment is divided as follows:

- **Assignments:** 10% (Various topics throughout the semester)
- **Paper Presentation:** 20% (Presentation on a chosen topic related to nanoelectronics)
- **Mid-Semester Examination:** 20%
- **End-Semester Examination:** 50%
