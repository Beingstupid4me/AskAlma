### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE340/CSE540/ECE340  
**Course Name:** Digital Image Processing  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate and Postgraduate students  

This course provides an in-depth exploration of digital image processing, covering fundamental theories, algorithms, and practical applications. Students will gain hands-on experience with essential techniques used in the field.

---

### Prerequisites  
- **Mathematics:** MTH100 (Calculus) and MTH201 (Probability & Statistics) are mandatory prerequisites.  
- **Signals & Systems:** ECE250 is a desirable prerequisite for those with an electrical engineering background.  
Other prerequisites may include foundational knowledge in computer science or engineering, which can be discussed with the instructor if needed.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
1. **CO1:** Demonstrate a thorough understanding of fundamental spatial domain image processing techniques.  
2. **CO2:** Analyze image transforms and apply them in both spatial and Fourier domains.  
3. **CO3:** Apply image processing techniques in spatial and frequency domains for compression purposes.  
4. **CO4:** Analyze and compare spatial and frequency domain properties relevant to image compression.  
5. **CO5:** Examine feature representation, segmentation, and morphological transformations in digital images.
