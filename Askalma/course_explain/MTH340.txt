**Course Overview:**  
Real Analysis-II is a 4-credit course designed for both undergraduate and postgraduate students. It builds on foundational calculus concepts by exploring advanced real analysis topics in depth.

---

### **Course Description**  
This course delves into the systematic development of real analysis, focusing on generalized settings such as real-valued functions. Key areas include:  
1. Properties of real numbers (LUB, GLB).  
2. Metric spaces and their properties (boundedness, compactness, connectedness).  
3. Sequences and series (convergence, Cauchy sequences, uniform continuity).  
4. Measure theory (Lebesgue measure, measurable functions, integration).  

**Learning Outcomes:**  
By the end of this course, students will:  
1. Understand real number properties and their applications.  
2. Construct rigorous proofs for analysis results.  
3. Analyze limits in sequences, series, differentiation, and integration.  
4. Apply measure theory to solve integration problems.
