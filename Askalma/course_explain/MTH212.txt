**Course Overview**

This course MTH212, named "Abstract algebra 1" provides a comprehensive introduction to abstract algebra, focusing on groups, rings, and fields. It is designed for undergraduate students in their second year, offering 4 credits. The course aims to build a strong foundation in these algebraic structures while emphasizing both theoretical understanding and practical problem-solving skills.

---

**Prerequisites**

- **Mandatory:** MTH 100 or equivalent (Linear Algebra)

---

**Learning Outcomes**

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- **CO1:** Explain algebraic properties of important families of groups.
- **CO2:** Classify groups up to isomorphism and understand their algebraic properties.
- **CO3:** Discuss algebraic properties of rings and fields (particularly finite fields) and classify them up to isomorphism.
- **CO4:** Understand the algebraic properties of rings and fields (including finite fields) and classify them up to isomorphism.
- **CO5:** Write formal mathematical proofs.

**Weekly Syllabus**

| Week Number | Lecture Topic                     | COs Met       | Assignments               |
|-------------|-----------------------------------|----------------|---------------------------|
| 1 & 2       | Equivalence relation and partition. Groups and subgroups, Cyclic groups, Abelian groups, Matrix groups, Quaternionic group. | CO1&4         | Tutorial Sheet 1          |
| 3           | Lagrange's theorem, normal subgroup, quotient groups, isomorphism theorems, Group actions. | CO1,2&4       | Tutorial Sheet 2          |
| 4-6         | Permutation groups, Simple groups, Simplicity of the alternating group. Burnside's lemma and its applications. | CO1,2&4       | Tutorial Sheet 3          |
| 7           | Centralizers and Normalizers, class equation and its applications. Dihedral Groups. | CO1,2&4       | Tutorial Sheet 4          |
| 8-9         | Sylow's Theorems and their applications. | CO1,2&4       | Tutorial Sheet 5          |
| 10          | Direct product of groups, Fundamental Theorem of Finite Abelian groups. | CO1,2&4       | Tutorial Sheet 6          |
| 11-13       | Rings and Fields, Matrix rings and Group rings, units and zero divisors, ideals, quotient rings, principal ideal, prime ideal, maximal ideal, nil and nilpotent ideals, integral domain. | CO3&4         | Tutorial Sheet 7          |
| 14-16       | Isomorphism of rings, Direct product of rings, Polynomial rings, The Chinese Remainder Theorem. | CO3&4         | Tutorial Sheet 8          |
| 17-19       | Principal ideal domain, Euclidean domain, Unique factorization domain (basic algebraic properties only). | CO3&4         | Tutorial Sheet 9          |
