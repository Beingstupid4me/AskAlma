# Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE563  
**Course Name:** Multimedia Computing and Applications  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate/Postgraduate (UG/PG)  

## Course Description  
By the end of this course, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of the core concepts and emerging technologies in multimedia computing. The curriculum is designed to cover advanced topics such as content-based retrieval, multimedia data processing, summarization, mining, and analysis. Students will also explore applications in social media, including event detection, sentiment analysis, and recommendation systems. Through hands-on assignments and projects, students will develop the ability to analyze, design, and evaluate multimedia systems. The course emphasizes both theoretical knowledge and practical implementation skills.

## Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  

- **CO1:** Understand advanced computing fundamentals in multimedia applications and social media contexts.  
- **CO2:** Apply techniques for content-based retrieval, summarization, and data mining across various multimedia platforms.  
- **CO3:** Analyze and evaluate algorithms for multimedia processing tasks such as classification, event detection, and sentiment analysis.  
- **CO4:** Design and implement multimedia systems to solve real-world problems in social media and beyond.
