### Course Overview  
The course BIO545, titled **BIOSTATISTICS** is a 4-credit elective course offered to students pursuing B-Tech and M-Tech programs. This course aims to provide students with a strong foundation in quantitative biology and statistical methods, enabling them to analyze biological data effectively and understand applied biostatistics concepts.

---

### Prerequisites  
Students are expected to have a basic understanding of **Biology** before enrolling in this course. The prerequisites include familiarity with fundamental biological concepts, which will be essential for grasping the statistical methods covered in this course.

---

### Course Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
1. Develop skills to solve statistical problems related to biological queries.  
2. Summarize theoretical and quantitative concepts such as Mendelian genetics, GWAS, eQTl analysis, and population genetics.  
3. Solve quantitative problems related to population genetics.

### Assessment Strategy  
The course assessment is designed to evaluate both theoretical understanding and practical application of concepts. The breakdown is as follows:  

- **Mid-Semester Examination (30%)**: Covers the first half of the course material, focusing on foundational concepts like statistical methods and data analysis techniques.  
- **End-Semester Examination (30%)**: Assesses comprehensive understanding of all topics covered during the course, including advanced concepts in population genetics and quantitative traits.  
- **Assignments/Labs/Tutorials (20%)**: Includes practical tasks and case studies to apply statistical methods to biological data.  
- **Quiz (20%)**: Regular quizzes to assess students' grasp of key concepts and theories discussed in lectures.

The mid-sem examination carries a significant weight because it evaluates the foundational knowledge necessary for understanding more complex topics later in the course.
