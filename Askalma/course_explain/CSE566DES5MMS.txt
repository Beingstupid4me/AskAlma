### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE566/DES5MMS  
**Course Name:** Mobile & Middleware Systems  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) students  

This course is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of middleware systems and their applications in various domains, including crowdsensing, public health, education, and more. The primary objective is to equip students with the knowledge and skills to design, build, and optimize mobile and middleware systems for handling unstructured data collected by mobile devices. Students will also gain insights into critical areas such as data privacy, data processing, analysis, and visualization.

---

### Prerequisites  
- **Mandatory:** Any 3rd, 4th year UG, Masters, or Ph.D. students.  
- **CSE535 Mobile Computing:** Students must have excellent programming skills with Kotlin/Java, GoLang/C#, Node/Rust, and proficiency in using GitHub and other relevant tools.

Additional prerequisites may be specified if needed based on the complexity of the course content.

---

### Course Outcomes (COs)  
The course aims to enable students to achieve the following outcomes:  

1. **CO1:** Students will develop a deep understanding of middleware systems and their applications in real-world scenarios.  
2. **CO2:** Students will be able to design and implement RESTful web services for mobile-based data collection and management.  
3. **CO3:** Students will be able to design and construct mobile database applications for unstructured data, ensuring efficient data handling and retrieval.  
4. **CO4:** Students will develop the ability to critically analyze contemporary research in middleware systems, critique existing designs, and propose alternative solutions or improvements.
