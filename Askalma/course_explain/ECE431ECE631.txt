### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** ECE431 / ECE631  
**Course Name:** Antennas Theory and Design  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) students with a background in fundamental electromagnetics.  

This course provides an introduction to the principles of antenna theory and design, complementing foundational knowledge in wave propagation. It combines theoretical concepts with practical applications, offering students the opportunity to design and test antennas. The curriculum is designed to equip students with the skills necessary to implement wireless systems, including communications and radar technologies.

---

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory Prerequisites:**  
- ECE230: Fields and Waves  

**Desirable Prerequisites (Other):** None specified.  

Students are expected to have a solid understanding of fundamental electromagnetics before enrolling in this course.

---

### Course Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- **CO1:** Analyze Maxwell's equations for wave propagation through different mediums (free space, lossy, dispersive).  
- **CO2:** Design and/or analyze wire, loop, aperture, active, and broadband antennas in theory or using computational electromagnetic software.  
- **CO3:** Extend the analysis and design from a single antenna to an antenna array.  
- **CO4:** Measure basic parameters of common antennas (e.g., input impedance, radiation pattern).
