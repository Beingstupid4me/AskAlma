### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE655  
**Course Name:** Network Science  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate/Postgraduate (UG/PG)  

#### Course Description:  
This course provides an introduction to network science, an emerging interdisciplinary field with applications in various domains such as social networks, biological systems, and infrastructure. The primary objective is to equip students with foundational knowledge of network theory, analytical tools, and practical skills to model, analyze, and understand complex networks.

---

### Prerequisites  
**Mandatory Prerequisites:**  
- Graph theory fundamentals, algorithms, and programming  

**Desirable Prerequisites:**  
- Graph theory fundamentals, algorithms, and programming (Note: This field is highly recommended for a strong foundation in network science concepts.)  

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs):  
1. **CO1:** Students will be able to explain basic concepts of network science, including random networks, scale-free networks, network evolution, robustness, and community structure.  
2. **CO2:** Students will be able to model and implement computations of network properties derived from empirical data.  
3. **CO3:** Students will be able to analyze and visualize networks effectively.  
4. **CO4:** Students will be able to design and tweak algorithms to address specific research or application questions in network science.
