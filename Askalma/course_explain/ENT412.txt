**Course Overview**

**Course Code:** ENT412  
**Course Name:** Entrepreneurial Khichadi  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** B.Tech. post 4 semesters (students from 3rd year), M.Tech., and Ph.D.  

This course is designed to introduce students to the fundamentals of entrepreneurship, equipping them with essential skills and knowledge to develop viable business ventures. The curriculum emphasizes team-based learning, practical case studies, and comprehensive assessments. Students will engage in weekly lectures, assignments, and presentations to achieve the following course outcomes.

**Prerequisites**

- **Mandatory:** None specified.
- **Desirable:** Basic understanding of business concepts is recommended but not mandatory.
- **Other:** None specified.

**Learning Outcomes (COs)**

1. **CO1:** Students will master key elements of entrepreneurship, including attributes like flexibility, ethical values, and problem-solving skills.
2. **CO2:** Students will develop proficiency in team dynamics, leadership, and aligning goals with business objectives.
3. **CO3:** Students will synthesize strategies for creating successful ventures, covering areas such as innovation, marketing, and financial management.
4. **CO4:** Students will demonstrate the ability to propose, analyze, and present comprehensive business plans tailored for entrepreneurial success.

**Weekly Lecture Plan**

- **Week 1:** Introduction to entrepreneurship and course outline.
- **Week 2:** Formation of student teams for class projects.
- **Week 3:** Leadership skills and core employee hiring strategies.
- **Week 4:** Creative thinking and innovation techniques.
- **Week 5:** Design and analysis of product prototypes.
- **Week 6:** Marketing strategies and competitive analysis.
- **Week 7:** Case studies and team project presentations.
- **Week 8:** Business model development and revenue models.
- **Week 9:** Financial planning and cash-flow management.
- **Week 10:** Intellectual property and legal aspects.
- **Week 11:** Investor pitch preparation and valuation methods.
- **Week 12:** Final business plan development.
- **Week 13:** Final presentations.

**Assessment Plan**

- **Attendance & Class Participation:** 20%
- **Weekly Classwork & Presentations:** 30%
- **Project Work & Business Plan:** 20%
- **Final Presentations:** 30%

**Resource Materials**

- **Textbooks:**
  - "Innovation and Entreprenuership" by Peter F. Drucker
  - "The Lean startup" by Eric Ries
  - "The Art of the Start" by Guy Kawasaki
  - "Business Model Generation" by Alexander Osterwalder, Yves Pigneur
  - "The Hard Thing about Hard Things" by Ben Horowitz
