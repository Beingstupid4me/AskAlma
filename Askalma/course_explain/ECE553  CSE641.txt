**Course Overview**

This course, **ECE553 / CSE 641: Deep Learning**, is designed to provide an in-depth understanding of advanced machine learning techniques with a focus on deep learning. The course spans 13 weeks and is suitable for both undergraduate and postgraduate students. It covers the theoretical foundations, practical applications, and hands-on implementation of deep learning models.

**Prerequisites**

The prerequisite for this course is **Machine Learning**, ensuring that students have a foundational understanding of machine learning concepts before delving into more complex topics.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- **CO1**: Understand and explain various deep learning models such as Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), Autoencoders, Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN), and Long Short-Term Memory networks (LSTM).  
- **CO2**: Analyze and evaluate the applications of deep learning in diverse domains, including computer vision, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning.  
- **CO3**: Design, implement, and deploy custom deep learning models to solve real-world problems of their choice.

### Assessment Strategy  
The course assessment is divided as follows:  
- **Assignments**: 50% of the final grade. These include coding assignments and reports on selected topics.  
- **Project**: 30% of the final grade. A group or individual project involving the development of a deep learning model will be required.  
- **Mid-sem Exam**: 10% of the final grade. This exam will cover the first half of the course material.  
- **End-sem Exam**: 10% of the final grade. The final exam will assess the overall understanding of deep learning concepts and applications.
