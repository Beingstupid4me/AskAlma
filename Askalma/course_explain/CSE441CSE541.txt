# Course Overview  
**Course Code:** CSE441/541  
**Course Name:** Advanced Biometrics  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate/Postgraduate (UG/PG)  

This course is designed to provide an in-depth understanding of advanced biometric systems and their applications. It covers the fundamental concepts, algorithms, and techniques used in biometric technologies, including face recognition, fingerprint analysis, and iris recognition. The course also integrates topics from machine learning, image processing, and pattern recognition to equip students with the necessary skills for designing and implementing biometric systems.

---

# Prerequisites  
**Mandatory Pre-requisite:** MTH201 Probability & Statistics  
**Desirable Pre-requisite:** CSE343/CSE543 Machine Learning  
**Other Pre-requisites:** Pattern Recognition or Machine Learning  

Students are expected to have a basic understanding of probability and statistics, as well as machine learning concepts. Exposure to pattern recognition techniques is highly recommended but not mandatory.

---

# Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
1. **CO1:** Understand the fundamental principles and algorithms used in biometric systems, including face, fingerprint, and iris recognition.  
2. **CO2:** Evaluate the performance of biometric systems using appropriate metrics and techniques.  
3. **CO3:** Design and implement biometric systems for real-world applications, considering factors such as accuracy, reliability, and security.  
4. **CO4:** Analyze and solve theoretical and practical problems related to biometrics, working independently or in groups.
