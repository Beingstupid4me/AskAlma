### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** RF Circuit Design (ECE321/ECE521)  
**Course Name:** Radio Frequency Circuit Design  
**Credits:** 4  
**Instructor:** Dr. M. S. Hashmi

---

### Prerequisites  
- **Foundations of Electromagnetic Fields and Waves**: This course instructed by Dr. M. S. Hashmi, is required for successful completion of RF Circuit Design. Students are expected to have a strong understanding of basic electromagnetic principles before enrolling.  

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
1. **CO1:** Understand the fundamental concepts of radio frequency (RF) circuit design and its applications in modern communication systems.  
2. **CO2:** Analyze and design passive and active RF circuits using transmission line theory, Smith charts, and software tools like ADS.  
3. **CO3:** Apply principles of impedance matching and tuning to optimize RF circuit performance.  
4. **CO4:** Design and analyze multi-port networks using ADS and other simulation tools.  
5. **CO5:** Develop skills in PCB manufacturing and measurement techniques for RF components, including the use of Vector Network Analyzers (VNAs).

### Assessment Strategy  
The assessment plan is designed to evaluate both theoretical knowledge and practical skills:  

- **Assignments:** Several assignment tasks will be given throughout the course, focusing on design challenges and problem-solving using ADS and other tools.  
- **Quizzes:** Random quizzes will assess students' understanding of key concepts covered in lectures.  
- **Midsem Exam (20%):** A mid-semester examination to evaluate students' grasp of fundamental topics.  
- **Final Exam (15%):** A comprehensive final exam covering the entire course content.  
- **Project (30%):** Students will work on a group project, which includes schematic design, layout, and simulation using ADS tools. The project will culminate in a presentation and written report.  

The end-semester examination carries a significant weight of 25%, reflecting its importance in evaluating overall understanding of the course material.

### Conclusion  
This course provides a comprehensive understanding of RF circuit design, covering both theoretical foundations and practical applications. Through hands-on exercises with ADS, PCB manufacturing, and VNAs, students will gain the skills needed to analyze and design RF circuits for modern communication systems. The assessment plan ensures that students achieve a well-rounded grasp of the subject matter, while the project component offers an opportunity to apply their knowledge in a real-world context.
