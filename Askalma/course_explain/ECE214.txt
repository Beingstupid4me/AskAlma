### Course Overview  
**Course Name:** Integrated Electronics: Circuits and Systems  
**Course Code:** ECE 214  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** Undergraduate Students (UG)  

This course provides a comprehensive understanding of electronic circuits and systems, focusing on the analysis and design of both analog and digital circuits. The curriculum begins with an overview of semiconductor technology, including Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs), Metal Oxide Field Effect Transistors (MOSFETs), and their respective models. Subsequent weeks delve into amplifiers, biasing techniques, power amplifier design, and advanced topics such as CMOS digital logic circuits and memory elements. The course integrates theoretical concepts with practical laboratory exercises to reinforce learning outcomes.

---

### Prerequisites  
- **Pre-requisite (Mandatory):** Digital Circuits (CSE111) or its equivalent  
- **Desirable:** Basic Electronics (ECE113)  
- **Other:** Introductory Engineering Design (DES130)  

Students are expected to have a foundational understanding of digital and analog electronics before enrolling in this course.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
- **CO1:** Derive small-signal models for BJTs and MOSFETs.  
- **CO2:** Apply biasing techniques to BJT and MOSFET circuits.  
- **CO3:** Analyze single-stage and multi-stage amplifier circuits.  
- **CO4:** Design and analyze CMOS digital logic circuits.
