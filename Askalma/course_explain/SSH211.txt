**Course Overview**

This course, titled "Theatre Appreciation" (Course Code: SSH211), is a 4-credit undergraduate program designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of theatre appreciation and its practical applications. The course spans an entire semester and is open to all undergraduates who meet the prerequisite requirements.

**Course Description**

"Theatre Appreciation" is structured to blend theoretical knowledge with hands-on experience. Students will explore the works of renowned theatre masters, including Jerzy Grotowsky, Badal Sircar, and Constantine Stanislavsky, while also engaging in practical exercises such as voice work, mime performances, and theatrical games. The course emphasizes the importance of communication skills and culminates in a series of assessments that evaluate both theoretical knowledge and practical performance.

**Prerequisites**

While there are no formal prerequisites for this course, students are expected to have an open mind and a strong desire to communicate effectively. This foundational skill is crucial for participating in class activities and performing on stage.

**Learning Outcomes (COs)**

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- **CO1**: Demonstrate a thorough understanding of the biographies and techniques proposed by theatre masters.
- **CO2**: Overcome inhibitions and enhance their communication abilities through various exercises and performances.
- **CO3**: Perform a mime show that showcases their ability to interpret and express theatrical techniques.
- **CO4**: Develop the skills necessary to perform a short play, demonstrating their understanding of contemporary Indian playwrights' works.

**Learning Resources**

Students are encouraged to utilize the following resources to enhance their learning experience:

- **Class Notes**: Detailed notes on theatre masters such as Jerzy Grotowsky, Badal Sircar, and Constantine Stanislavsky, along with analyses of plays like "Les Fourberies de Scapin" by Molière, "Ghasi Ram Kotwal" by Vijay Tendulkar, and one-act plays by authors such as Saki, Josephina Niggli, Oliphant Down, Kenneth Lillington, and Rabindranath Tagore.
- **Textbook**: *Theatre of the West* by Molière
- **Reference Book**: *Classroom Experiments in Theatre* by Badal Sircar

This course is designed to provide a well-rounded understanding of theatre appreciation while fostering practical skills through hands-on exercises and performances.
