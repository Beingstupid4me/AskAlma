### Course Overview  
**Course Code:** DES102  
**Course Name:** Introduction to HCI (IHCI)  
**Credits:** 4  
**Offered To:** All UG 1st Year  
**Course Description:** This course provides a theoretical and practical understanding of human-computer interaction (HCI) design, focusing on user-centered and design thinking, usability, interfaces, rapid prototyping, and evaluation. It is project-based, allowing students to work on user-centric prototypes.  

---

### Prerequisites  
- **Mandatory Prerequisites:** None specified.  
- **Desirable Prerequisites:** Basic understanding of computer systems and familiarity with digital tools.  
- **Other Prerequisites:** Students are expected to have a foundational knowledge of programming concepts.  

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
1. **CO1:** Students will be able to discuss and distinguish between human-computer interaction, user experience design, and design thinking.  
2. **CO2:** Students will be able to apply user-centered design techniques, data gathering methods, rapid prototyping, and effective communication of design concepts.  
3. **CO3:** Students will be able to assess the usefulness and usability of interaction designs through evaluation metrics.  
4. **CO4:** Students will be able to ideate, prototype, and evaluate new design concepts as part of collaborative group projects.

### Assessment Strategy  
**Types of Evaluation:**  
- Assignments: 40% (5 quizzes at 8 points each)  
- Project: 35% (Deliverables: Basic working prototype, Prototype evaluation plan, Prototype evaluation report, Final presentation - Personal Report)  
- End-Semester Examination: 25%  

**Contribution to Grade:**  
The assessment plan is designed to evaluate both theoretical understanding and practical application. The quizzes assess foundational knowledge, while the project evaluates design thinking and usability skills. The end-semester examination will comprehensively cover all course objectives.
