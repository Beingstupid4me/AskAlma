### Course Overview  
This course  "Statistical Machine Learning" (Course Code: CSE342/CSE542/ECE356/ECE556) is designed to provide an in-depth understanding of statistical machine learning and pattern classification. It combines theoretical concepts with practical applications, emphasizing hands-on experience through programming assignments and a project component. The course is suitable for both undergraduate and postgraduate students pursuing degrees in computer science or related fields.

---

### Prerequisites  
Students are expected to have a foundational knowledge in the following areas:  
- **Mandatory:** A solid understanding of probability and statistics (MTH201).  
- **Desirable:** Basic programming skills, preferably in Python (CSE101).  
Additional prerequisites or resources may be required based on individual student needs.

---

### Learning Outcomes (COs)  
By the end of this course, students will be able to:  
1. **CO1** Understand and explain fundamental concepts in machine learning and pattern classification.  
2. **CO2** Apply suitable techniques for feature extraction and classification to solve real-world problems.  
3. **CO3** Design and evaluate machine learning algorithms for various applications.  
4. **CO4** Implement and test classifiers on diverse datasets, focusing on performance evaluation.  
5. **CO5** Analyze and interpret results to improve the effectiveness of machine learning models.

### Conclusion  
This course offers a comprehensive exploration of statistical machine learning and pattern classification, blending theoretical knowledge with practical implementation. The assessment strategy ensures a balanced evaluation of both theoretical understanding and practical skills.
