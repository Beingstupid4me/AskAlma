The Centre for Intelligent Product Development (CIPD) serves as a leading endeavor which pursues independent product development and innovation. A collaborative group of experts with research and industrial experience joins forces at the lab to produce advanced solutions which serve market needs and decrease both design costs and risks significantly. CIPD directs its efforts toward developing India’s independence in high-tech electronics production through domestic design innovations beyond assembly and packaging operations. Similar to other nations CIPD seeks to lower India’s dependency on electronic imports which in turn supports economic development as well as administrative systems.

CIPD follows a three-part strategic direction that first concentrates on building product development expert capabilities to create technologically advanced competitive products through human resources integration and second focuses on streamlining product manufacturing sequences from conception to production but also third supports domestic manufacturing of high-volume mature technology products toward sustainability. The CIPD builds international leadership through its approach to connect industry and academia along with developing technology platforms that achieve global certification standards to develop India’s position within the global product design and electronics sector.

The production challenges of CIPD guarantee manufacturing ecosystem growth which supports the strengthening of India’s manufacturing sector. The center advances technological progress through research-driven innovation while working together with industries to stay ahead in cutting-edge technological development which strongly affects India’s economic development and industrial structure
