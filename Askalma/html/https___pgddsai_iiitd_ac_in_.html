PROGRAM OVERVIEW

We provide the opportunity to prepare for life

Campus

A coveted institute for IT career seekers. IIIT-D was established in 2008. The Institute has state of the art infrastructure and facilities and is housed in its own permanent 25-acre campus in Okhla.

Departments and Research Centers

The institute has 6 departments; CSE, ECE, CB, HCD, SSH and Mathematics. Five Research Centres; ICAI, CCB, CDNM, CTP, CSRC.

Faculty

Over 60 regular faculty and more than 20 visiting faculty members, all PhD's from the world’s leading institutes.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

  • Foundational statistics, mathematical analysis, and optimization
  • Unsupervised and supervised machine learning and allied data analysis techniques
  • Solving real-life problems arising from different industry verticals
  • How to explore data leveraging visualization techniques and useful summary statistics
  • Basic Extraction Transformation and Loading (ETL) of data
  • Theoretical knowledge and intricacies of techniques commonly used in business analytics
  • Indepth understanding of various business application domains

WHO SHOULD APPLY

  • Students who have completed Graduation/Post graduation in Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics and intend to pursue a career in Data Sciences and AI
  • IT and other professionals with limited or no knowledge in artificial intelligence and want to make a career change.
  • Policymakers, senior-level executives, and not for profit leaders who recognize the importance of AI and want to harness its potential responsibly
  • Decision makers — from any size organization, any industry, and any country — who work with internal or external technology teams
