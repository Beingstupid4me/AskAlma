professors, research assistants, budding engineers, and more

Are dedicated to solving real-life artificial intelligence problems and expanding the horizon of science for the benefit of humanity.

  • In the autumn of 2016, the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) joined forces with Infosys Limited to establish the Infosys Centre for Artificial Intelligence (CAI) on the IIIT Delhi campus. The center focuses on research and development in the fields of autonomous vehicles, AI in healthcare, AI in robotics, AI in mobility, and satellite navigation.
  • Since its inception, the members of CAI have made significant contributions towards creating a global impact through ongoing research and developments. The center has successfully produced several products, including the Autonomous Last Mile Vehicle.
