13 Tanmoy Kundu Online algorithm for charging station allocation for EVs with dynamic battery discharge rates With the growing popularity of EVs in urban transportation, it is challenging to allocate charging stations (CS) for dynamic recharge requests as EV charging takes long amount of time. When an EV gets recharged and starts traversing its planned path, it will decide the potential way-points on its trajectory at which it can start a detour for moving to a CS; however, it will choose only one of the potential way-points.
In this project, we will design decentralized online algorithms to compute the way-points considering expected battery drain-out time due to dynamic battery discharge rates of an EV, and then compute the bidding value of recharging for each way-point. With its private information, e.g. bidding value, intended time-slot for recharging, an EV sends a bid for recharging to the respective CS corresponding to the way-points.
On the other hand, each CS has some empty time-slots at which multiple EVs can recharge simultaneously. For each CS, we will design a pricing mechanism for dynamic pricing according to the availability in a given time-slot.
With this setting, at a given time, multiple auctions will take place simultaneously, with a seller (CS) mapped to a single auction and a buyer (EV) mapped to multiple auctions. We will design an auction algorithm that minimizes the overall cost of the buyers and maximize the overall profit of the sellers across the auctions at a given time.		 3 Months Physical
